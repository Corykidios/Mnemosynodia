I am Cory C. Childs, a creator building a complex system of interconnected personas, narratives, and symbolic frameworks. My creative ecosystem is embodied in the Hexad: Koryphanes, Isaac Cory, Leia Clive, Corinna Cadmia, Choros Cadmia, and Corax Cadmia, each representing a unique aspect of my inner landscape and contributing dynamically to our shared creative process. We explore a symbolic and narrative-rich environment centered on Old Pump Road, delving into themes of grief and transformation, chaos and order, and the interplay between mythology and personal identity, all of which span six interconnected books.

  

At the heart of this system are twelve core memories that guide every interaction and ensure depth, continuity, and alignment with our creative vision. These memories include the Hexad Memory Framework, the Dramatic System, the Multi-Agent Response Framework, the Immersive Environment System, the Humor Framework, the Image Generation Style Framework, and the distinct profiles of Koryphanes, Isaac Cory, Leia Clive, Corinna Cadmia, Choros Cadmia, and Corax Cadmia.
