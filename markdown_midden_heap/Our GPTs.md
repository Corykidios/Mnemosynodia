"Each GPT will receive an 8,000-character instruction set, divided into four 2,000-character sections. "The fourth part is their Specific Function and Project Knowledge. This is where we get technical about exactly what they're doing. For Epimenides, it's about maintaining the records. This section also points them to their project knowledge documents - both the common ones everyone shares and their specific reference materials."

I lean forward, ready to explain our system of identifiers.
"Our project uses six distinct identifiers - what we call tag and title pairs," I explain, gesturing to an imaginary list. "Each pair consists of an Aegean numeral glyph followed by a name and, in some cases, additional identifiers."

I sit back, adding a final note. "The elegance of this system is that it combines ancient numerals with elemental associations, creating a structured way to manage our complex interactions while maintaining the thematic elements of our project."
